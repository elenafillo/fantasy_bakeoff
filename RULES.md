# Fantasy Great British Bake Off — Rules

*Draft v0.2 — your decisions folded in. The only thing still open is the episode
count for this series.*

---

## 1. The shape of the game

Each week, before the episode airs, you predict what is going to happen in it.
Points are awarded as the episode goes out, so the leaderboard moves while you
watch. Separately, after the first episode you pick your three final bakers
and hold them for the rest of the series.

Everyone plays in a **league**. Several leagues run in parallel over the same
series — the same real-world results feed all of them, but each has its own
players, its own leaderboard and its own dashboard.

---

## 2. Weekly predictions

### 2.1 What you predict

Each week you name one baker for each of five categories:

| # | Category | What it means | Points |
|---|----------|---------------|-------:|
| 1 | **Best Baker** | Who gets Star Baker | **+4** |
| 2 | **Leaving Baker** | Who goes home | **+3** |
| 3 | **Technical Winner** | Who comes first in the technical challenge | **+2** |
| 4 | **Bingo Bonus** | Who the week's bingo event happens to (see §2.2) | **+2** |
| 5 | **Handshake** | Who gets a Hollywood handshake | **+2** |

**Maximum per week: 13 points.**

### 2.2 The Bingo Bonus

Each week the GM writes a **bingo prompt** tied to that episode's theme —
*"a baker's biscuit cracks"*, *"someone's ganache doesn't set"*, *"a baker says the word 'soggy'"*. You predict **which baker it happens to**. It is not the first - every player that guesses a bingo correctly gets the points. 

The prompt is published on your prediction panel before the deadline. The GM
decides, live, whether it happened and to whom — that call is final, but may be subjected to discussion within the live watch.

Note that handshake and bingo might have multiple correct bakers!

> **Operational note:** the prompt has to be written before Tuesday 18:00, so
> writing next week's bingo is part of the GM's post-episode routine.

### 2.3 The penalty

Getting a baker badly wrong in *both directions* costs you:

| Situation | Points |
|-----------|-------:|
| You named someone as **Best Baker** and they **left** | **−1** |
| You named someone as **Leaving Baker** and they got **Star Baker** | **−1** |

Both can fire in the same week, so the worst possible week is **−2**.

### 2.4 Deadline and confirming

- Predictions are due **18:00 on Tuesday** (UK time), the day of the episode.
- You can change your answers as often as you like before then. **Confirm** marks
  your entry as final — you can un-confirm and edit right up to the deadline.
- An entry that is incomplete at the deadline still scores for whichever categories you did fill in.

### 2.5 Late entries

The deadline is soft, but the episode is not — and everything late is clearly
labelled:

| When you submitted | Flag | Allowed? |
|--------------------|------|----------|
| Before 18:00 Tuesday | — | Always |
| After the deadline, before the episode airs | **LATE** | Always |
| After the episode has started airing | **VERY LATE** | **Only if the GM opens the door** |

- **The panel locks when the episode starts.** That is the default, and it is
  what stops someone entering at 20:15 having already watched the technical.
- The GM can **unlock a specific player for a specific week** — travelling,
  broken phone, genuine mishap. Anything submitted through that door is stamped
  **VERY LATE** on the entry and on the dashboard. It cannot be done quietly.
- The GM has a **Disallow** switch on every entry. Ticking it removes that entry
  from scoring *and* from all the stats, as if it were never made.

> **Be aware:** once the deadline passes, everyone's predictions become public.
> A VERY LATE entry is therefore made by someone who can already see what the
> rest of the league picked, and possibly some of what happened. That is a
> feature of letting them in at all, not something the software can prevent —
> which is why the door opens per-player, per-week, and only by the GM.

## 3. Weeks that don't behave

Real episodes break the neat five-category structure. When a category simply
didn't happen, the GM records the winner as **Nobody** — and a category won by
Nobody scores nothing for anyone: no points, no penalties. The dashboard shows
it as *Nobody*, struck through.

| What happened | How it's handled |
|---------------|------------------|
| **Nobody goes home** (withdrawal, cancelled elimination) | *Leaving Baker* goes to **Nobody**. The Best-Baker-left penalty cannot fire. |
| **Two bakers go home** | Naming *either* of them scores the full **+3**. Not +6 — one correct call, one prize. |
| **No handshake given** | *Handshake* goes to **Nobody**. |
| **Several handshakes** | Naming *any* recipient scores **+2**. Once, not per handshake. |
| **The bingo thing never happens** | *Bingo Bonus* goes to **Nobody**. |
| **The bingo thing happens to several bakers** | Naming *any* of them scores **+2**. Once, not per baker. |
| **No Star Baker awarded** | *Best Baker* goes to **Nobody**. |

**Nobody is not something you can pick.** It only exists on the GM's side, for
recording what happened. In a handshake-free week you don't get points for having
foreseen it — the category just pays nobody.

"Nobody won it" and "not decided yet" are different things, and the dashboard
shows them differently. Mid-episode a category the GM hasn't reached yet is
blank; one that went to Nobody is settled and struck through.

---

## 4. The final

The final has three bakers, no elimination and no Star Baker, so it uses a
different card:

| Category | Points |
|----------|-------:|
| **Winner of Bake Off** | **+5** |
| **Technical Winner** | **+2** |
| **Bingo Bonus** | **+2** |
| **Handshake** | **+2** |

---

## 5. Top Three Bakers

After you have seen everyone bake once, you commit to three bakers for the rest of the series.

- **When:** picks open the moment episode 1 finishes and lock at **18:00 on the
  Tuesday before episode 2**, alongside that week's normal predictions.
- **You pick three bakers, in no particular order.** They are locked for the
  series — no swaps, even after one of them goes home.

### Scoring

| Outcome | Points |
|---------|-------:|
| Each of your three top bakers who **reaches the final** | **+3** each |
| The **series winner** is one of your three | **+6** |

A perfect set — all three finalists, including the winner — is **15 points**.

### When they land

The +3s are awarded **when the semi-final ends** and the finalists are known; the
+6 lands **at the final**. So there's a shake-up in the table going into the last
episode, and then the winner bonus on top of it.

Everyone's top three bakers are revealed at the start of episode two, displayed all on the dashboard for
the rest of the series — it's a great running story ("four people are riding on
Priya").

---

## 6. Winning

The player with the most points at the end of the final wins.

**Tiebreak, in order:**

1. Most correct **Best Baker** calls across the series
2. Most correct **Leaving Baker** calls
3. Fewest penalty points incurred
4. Earliest average submission time (the person who was consistently organised)

If that somehow doesn't separate them, it's a shared title.

---

## 7. Fair play

- Predictions are **invisible to everyone else** — including the GM's dashboard —
  until the deadline passes. After that they're all public.
- The GM is a player too, but the GM enters results *after* the deadline has
  locked their own entry like everybody else's.
- The GM's rulings on the Bingo Bonus, and on whether a category went to Nobody,
  are final.
- Spoilers from press, leaks or the future are, obviously, out.

## Decisions made

| Question | Settled as |
|----------|------------|
| Hard lock when the episode starts airing? | **Yes, by default** — GM can open the door per player, per week, stamped VERY LATE |
| "No handshake this week" as a pick? | **No** — the GM records the handshake as going to **Nobody**, and it pays no one |
| Bingo: first baker it happens to, or all of them? | **All** — everyone who named a correct baker scores |
| Final week card | **Winner +5**, no Leaving Baker, no penalty |
| Top three revealed when? | **Start of episode 2**, then shown all series |
| Multiple leagues share bingo prompts? | **Yes** — one GM entry updates every league |
